MOT Testing

From
£45

As a DVSA-authorised test centre, we carry out MOT testing for Class 4 vehicles (cars, taxis and light goods vehicles up to 3,000kg). Our fully qualified testers carry out every check to the letter — and we'll walk you through any advisories or failures in plain English, not garage-speak.

If your car fails and we carry out the repair work here, your retest is free of charge. We also offer a free vehicle health check with every MOT so you leave with a complete picture of your car's condition.

  • Full DVSA-standard MOT inspection
  • Brakes, steering & suspension checked
  • Lights, horn & wipers tested
  • Exhaust emissions test
  • Tyres & wheels inspected
  • Free retest if we fix the failure
  • Same-day results
  • Digital MOT certificate

Full Service

From
£120

Our full service covers over 60 inspection and replacement points and is recommended annually or every 12,000 miles — whichever comes first. Designed to keep your vehicle running safely, reliably and efficiently, it's the most comprehensive routine maintenance you can give your car.

Every full service includes a written report of what was checked, what was replaced and the current condition of all key components. No work is ever carried out without your approval.

  • Engine oil & filter replacement
  • Air & cabin filter check/replace
  • All fluid levels topped up
  • Brake fluid condition tested
  • Battery & charging system tested
  • Tyre condition & pressures checked
  • Suspension & steering inspection
  • Full written health check report
Book Full Service — from £120

Brake Inspection & Repair

Inspection
FREE

Your brakes are the most important safety system on your car. We offer a free, no-obligation brake inspection on every visit and will never recommend work you don't genuinely need. If you hear squealing, grinding or your car is pulling to one side when braking — don't wait.

Repair pricing depends on the work required. We'll always give you a fixed quote before touching the car, and we use only quality parts — no budget pads that fade after 3,000 miles.

  • Free visual & measurement inspection
  • Disc & pad wear measured
  • Caliper function checked
  • Brake fluid condition tested
  • Handbrake adjustment checked
  • Fixed price before any repair work
